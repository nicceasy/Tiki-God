# Notice: recipe data and sources

The MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers everything written for this project:
- the code;
- the drawings and their catalogue;
- the documentation and history write-ups;
- the curated data files;
- the generated statistics.

**Recipes are facts.** A drink's ingredients, amounts, method and glass are recorded here as facts, with credit to their creators and sources, as in the `creator`, `venue`, `source` and `source_urls` fields. Descriptions and notes in the data are written fresh. No book, website or dataset prose is copied.

**Imported slices.** Two slices of `data/drinks/` were extracted with the importers in `scripts/import/`. Only structured recipe facts were extracted.
- `books.json` comes from the book transcriptions in [SBoudrias/cocktails](https://github.com/SBoudrias/cocktails). That dataset is AGPL-3.0; none of its prose is used.
- `difford.json` comes from a scrape of [Difford's Guide](https://www.diffordsguide.com/) published at [LauraMarby/IA-SRI-SIM_Project](https://github.com/LauraMarby/IA-SRI-SIM_Project).

If you redistribute those two files on their own, check that your use fits their sources' terms. The rest of the catalogue was researched from the books, menus and articles listed in the [README](README.md).

**Names and marks.** Drink, bar, brand and product names, among them Trader Vic's, Don the Beachcomber, Pusser's, Mai-Kai, Tiki Farm and Libbey, belong to their owners. They are used here only to describe drinks and vessels, with no endorsement implied.
